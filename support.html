Support

Need help? Check the answers below, or contact us. We usually reply within 2 business days.

I'm coming from Microsoft Lens. How do I bring my scans over?

Lens usually saved PDFs to OneDrive (Documents › Office Lens or Microsoft Lens) and images to Photos. Install the OneDrive app and sign in, then in the Files app tap Browse › ••• › Edit and turn on OneDrive. In Tidyscan, tap the folder button next to Scan, open OneDrive, select your PDFs and tap Open. Each PDF becomes a searchable, named document. Images can be imported with the Photos button.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Tidyscan › Settings › Manage subscription, or go to iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Tidyscan Pro › Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Go to Settings › Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Family Sharing members also get Pro.

A total, tax amount or expiry date is wrong

Open the document › Details and edit it. Faded or crumpled receipts read best when flat, in good light, with the Auto or B&W filter.

My documents aren't syncing between devices

Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on each device and that iCloud is on for Tidyscan (Settings › [your name] › iCloud). Large scans can take a few minutes to upload.

How do I add the widgets, Lock Screen or Control Center buttons?

  • Home Screen: touch and hold the Home Screen › Edit › Add Widget › Tidyscan.
  • Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen › Customize › Lock Screen › add Tidyscan.
  • Control Center / Action Button: open Control Center › + › Add a Control › Tidyscan, or Settings › Action Button › Controls › Tidyscan.
  • Siri: say "Scan a document with Tidyscan".

How do I open a document from iPhone search?

Swipe down on the Home Screen and type any word that appears in the document. Vault documents are never shown in search.

I forgot the password of a PDF I shared

Tidyscan doesn't store PDF passwords. Share the document again from Tidyscan with a new password.
