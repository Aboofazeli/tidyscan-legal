Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

The short version: Tidyscan does not collect, sell or share your personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your scans are processed on your device and stored on your devices and in your own private iCloud.

This policy explains how the Tidyscan app for iPhone and iPad ("Tidyscan", "the app") handles information. Tidyscan is published by Ali Reza Aboofazeli ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

None. We do not operate servers and we do not receive your documents or any information about you or how you use the app. Tidyscan contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

2. Your documents

Documents you scan or import (images, recognised text, titles, folders, amounts, dates and expiry dates) are stored on your device. Text recognition, document classification, naming and receipt reading all happen on your device. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, optional smart naming uses Apple's on-device language model; your text is not sent to us or to any third party.

If you are signed in to iCloud, your documents sync between your devices through Apple's CloudKit, using the private database of your iCloud account. Only you can access it; we cannot. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Camera, photos and files

The camera is used only to scan documents when you tap Scan. Photos and files are accessed only when you pick them, using Apple's system pickers; Tidyscan does not get access to the rest of your library.

4. Face ID and the vault

App lock and the vault use Apple's LocalAuthentication. Tidyscan never sees or stores your face data or passcode. Vault documents are excluded from iPhone search (Spotlight).

5. iPhone search (Spotlight)

If "Show in iPhone search" is on, titles and recognised text of non-vault documents are added to your device's on-device search index so you can find them from the Home Screen. You can turn this off in Tidyscan › Settings, which removes them from the index.

6. Signatures and contacts

Your saved signature is stored only on the device where you drew it and is not synced. "Add to Contacts" for business cards opens Apple's contact card; nothing is saved unless you tap Done.

7. Notifications

Expiry and trial reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Tidyscan does not use a push-notification server.

8. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

9. Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete: deleting a document in Tidyscan removes it from the device and from your iCloud.
  • Export: share any document as PDF, images or text, and receipts as CSV.
  • iCloud: you can also manage or delete app data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. This applies to rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, PIPEDA, the CCPA/CPRA and similar laws.

10. Children

Tidyscan is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/Aboofazeli/tidyscan-legal/issues or see the Support page. Please don't attach documents or personal details.
